## Macro Timing (Outskirts Fritz)

The opening follows Universal up to **Skill 3**. After Skill 3:

1. **Camera Down (Universal animation wait)**: Ctrl, drag the camera downward for **2 seconds** by default, then Ctrl again. This preserves Universal's wait for Skill 3 to finish.
2. **Skill 1**: press once after the camera-down wait.
3. **Wait 1 second** (editable in Outskirts Fritz > Edit Configs).
4. **Skill 4**: use Universal's Ability 4 spam duration and interval.
5. Continue with Universal's existing **Shift and post-Shift** routine.

To change camera timing, open **Settings > Configs > Outskirts Fritz > Edit Configs** and edit **Skill 3 animation / Camera down time** or **Camera down pixels per step**. These values are independent of the Universal macro settings.
