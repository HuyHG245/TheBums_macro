## Raid opening
1. Wait for Acoustic Shells in slot 2. Its icon starts the raid routine.
2. Dash forward once by double-tapping the configured Dash key.
3. Wait 2 seconds, then press Skill 5 until the Heatwave icon confirms Colossal Shift.
4. Press Skill 4 once after Shift confirmation. When Heatwave first disappears and returns, leave it enabled. Press Skill 4 again only after the next stable disappearance and return. Skill 5, 3 and 1 each use their own timer; when ready together, they fire in that order.

## Chests and cutscenes
The macro finds the left OPEN button automatically. Finish is enabled in the routine only
after the left OPEN button clears and the chest selection screen is visible again.
Enable Open Emperor’s Trove under Edit configs to try the right OPEN once with an Emperor Key.
A missing or unresponsive optional chest is reported in Activity. While Skip is visible,
the Heatwave tracker discards pending icon transitions and waits for the combat HUD.
