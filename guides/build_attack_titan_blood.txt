## Required Titan: Colossal Titan
**Colossal Titan is mandatory** for this Raid macro. Equip Colossal Titan before starting so Skill 5 shifts to the required form and the post-Shift Heatwave/Skills 5, 3, and 1 routine is available.

## Required Build
Use a build with **Acoustic Shells in Skill Slot 2** and **Titan Shifting in Skill Slot 5**
before transforming. The other three pre-Shift skill slots are not used by this route.
After Shift, use the **same Colossal Titan skill layout** as the Fritz Raid guide.
This opening does not require Fritz's Flashbang, camera or directional positioning combo.

## Auto Skip Cutscenes
In **Settings > Macros > Attack Titan Raid Blood Awakening > Edit configs**,
**Auto skip cutscenes** can be turned ON or OFF. When enabled, the macro finds `skip.png`
and presses E at the start of the Raid or during phase transitions.

## Raid Automation
The macro starts when `acoustic_shells.png` is detected on the Skill 2 HUD slot.

1. Immediately press **Skill 2 — Acoustic Shells**.
2. **Forward Dash × 1:** double-tap **Space, Space** (or the Dash key configured in Keybinds).
3. Repeatedly press **Skill 5 — Titan Shifting** until `heatwave.png` confirms
   that Colossal Titan has shifted successfully. **No automatic timeout**.
4. If Phase 1 cutscene appears, optionally skip it with E.
5. During Colossal combat, Skill 4 **Heatwave** uses the same two-cycle icon detection
   as Fritz; **Skills 5, 3 and 1** run on independent timers until the CHESTS screen.
6. Raider's Chest is ON by default; Emperor's Trove is OFF by default.
   Use the Chest 1/Chest 2 **Pick spot** controls in Settings > Macros.
7. After the real Retry button is detected, the existing results, loot and Retry system
   handles the end of the Raid. Missing Heatwave by itself never means the Raid failed.

## Shared After-Shift Setup
The Colossal Titan abilities, Heatwave behavior, Phase 1 cutscene handling, chest
collection and Retry flow are the **same implementation used by Attack Titan Raid Fritz**.
Configure independent post-Shift Skill 1, 3 and 5 intervals in this mode's Edit configs.

## Loot and Retry
The macro detects **Primordial Evil** with `loot_attack_vessel.png` and
**Attack Serum** with `loot_attack_serum.png` at the result screen.
The Retry watchdog continues monitoring while Skill 5 is being spammed.
The program does not enter Recovery just because Shift was not confirmed.

## Edit Configs and Saving
Select this macro under **Settings > Macros > Abilities**, then click **Edit configs**.
The editor opens this mode's own cutscene/chest/Shift/Colossal settings and only its
applicable shared inputs. Your changes are stored separately in `config.json` and
restored when you reopen the app. Other macros' settings are not overwritten.
